## Personalised health recommendations
- **Gradual weight loss will lower health risk** [Weight]: Aim for 0.25-0.5 kg/week. A healthy range for your height is 47.4-58.6 kg. Even a 5% loss improves blood sugar, BP and cholesterol.
- **Drink about 2.4 litres of fluid daily** [Hydration]: Spread it across the day; start with a glass of water on waking. Buttermilk, coconut water and unsweetened tea count; sugary drinks do not.
- **Target 8,000-10,000 steps/day and 150 min of exercise per week** [Activity]: Mix brisk walking with 2-3 strength-training sessions per week to protect muscle while changing weight.
- **Sleep 7-9 hours** [Sleep]: Poor sleep raises hunger hormones and cravings. Keep a consistent schedule and finish dinner 2-3 hours before bed.
- **Avoid crash diets** [Goal: weight loss]: Your calorie target is a moderate deficit. Going far below it can cost muscle, energy and nutrients. Re-weigh weekly and adjust every 2-4 weeks.
- **Watch vitamin B12 and omega-3** [Nutrients]: B12 is scarce in plant foods - fortified foods or a supplement are usually needed (confirm with a blood test). Add flaxseed, chia or walnuts for omega-3.
- **Spread protein across all meals** [Eating habits]: Include a protein source (dal, curd, paneer, eggs, tofu, chicken, fish) at every meal for fullness and muscle maintenance.
- **Eat slowly and fill half your plate with vegetables** [Eating habits]: Mindful eating and vegetable-first plates reduce overeating without counting every calorie.
